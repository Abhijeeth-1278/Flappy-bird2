# 🦅 Flappy Crow 3D — The Obsidian Gauntlet

A fast-paced, gothic 3D corvid flight game built with **Three.js** and the **Web Audio API**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Procedural 3D Crow Model**:
  - Detailed corvid anatomy: sleek obsidian body, curved hooked beak, nasal bristles, dark glossy eyes with catchlights, tucked talons.
  - Multi-segmented articulated wings with primary feather blades that dynamically flap and flex based on aerodynamics.
  - Layered fan-tail feathers that tilt with pitch angle during climbs and dives.
  - Dynamic 3D rim lighting that casts subtle spectral/violet sheen across the crow's feathers.

- **Multiple Dynamic Camera Perspectives** (Press `C` or click Camera button):
  1. **Chase Cam** (Default): Third-person chase camera positioned behind the crow looking down the 3D gauntlet.
  2. **Side Cam (2.5D)**: Nostalgic Flappy Bird side view rendered with full 3D lighting, depth, and scrolling landscape.
  3. **Cockpit View**: High-adrenaline first-person flight directly through the crow's eyes!

- **4 Atmospheric Visual Themes** (Press `T` or click Theme button):
  - 🌌 **Twilight Mist**: Moody dusk purples, fog, and violet runic pillars.
  - 🌙 **Midnight Realm**: Deep indigo sky, cratered 3D moon, and sapphire runes.
  - 🌅 **Golden Dawn**: Warm amber sunrise, sunbeams, and terracotta stone arches.
  - 🩸 **Blood Eclipse**: Dark crimson skies and sinister ruby monoliths.

- **Crow Lore & Shiny Trinket Mechanic**:
  - Crows love shiny treasures! Floating gold coins and azure soul gems spawn in the archway gaps.
  - Collecting trinkets awards bonus points (`+3` each) and triggers custom sparkle particle explosions.

- **Procedural Web Audio Engine** (Zero external audio files / zero 404s):
  - Authentic crow "Caw! Caw!" vocal synthesis via dual formant bandpass filters & rasp modulator.
  - Aerodynamic wing-flap air whooshes.
  - Crystal bell score chimes and trinket pickup arpeggios.
  - Collision impact sound and procedural ambient wind drone.

- **Progress & Ranks**:
  - Saves your best high score in `localStorage`.
  - Corvid Ranks based on performance: *Fledgling*, *Clever Jackdaw*, *Obsidian Rook*, *Shadow Raven*, and *Corvid King 👑*.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flap / Jump** | <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>W</kbd>, or <kbd>↑</kbd> | Click or Tap anywhere |
| **Switch Camera** | <kbd>C</kbd> | Click Camera button |
| **Change Theme** | <kbd>T</kbd> | Click Theme button |
| **Toggle Audio** | <kbd>M</kbd> | Click Speaker icon |
| **Pause / Resume** | <kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Click Pause icon |
